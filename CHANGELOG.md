# Changelog

## 1.0.0-public-alpha.2

The workstation now runs inside VST3 hosts. This release adds two SUPER KNOBBER devices, project-synced scale assignments, editable musical boundaries, score/piano-roll editing, audio mix export, track and clip bounce, SVP export, per-device bypass and MIDI CC learning. Installation includes separate native Mac packages for Apple silicon and Intel. [Changes, platform information and known limits](docs/releases/v1.0.0-public-alpha.2.md)

## 1.0.0-public-alpha.1

Initial numbered public alpha with 38 VST3s, OSTF 5.2 support and live MIDI takes. [Release notes](docs/releases/v1.0.0-public-alpha.1.md)
