These are rule-based options, not a single correct answer. Chord voicing, melody emphasis, rhythm and style matter. Warnings do not forbid deliberate passing notes. No notes are quantized or rewritten.

Melody checks inspect concrete, enabled notes in explicitly identified melody tracks. They do not expand generated, referenced or looping phrases. Voice-leading arrows illustrate nearest chord-tone possibilities, not a complete arranged voicing.
