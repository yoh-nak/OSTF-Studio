## What's in alpha 4

- Open OSTF 5.2 scores and projects. Melody and lyric tracks have their own arrangement group. OSTF 5.1 takes and comp selections travel with the score.
- In Live Session, choose a take per track, queue sections with repeat counts, and see the current and upcoming chords while playing. Audition a cell or adopt its take combination in the arrangement.
- Use Scale Editor to audition scales with a VST3 instrument, compare candidates and write assignments to an OSTF file. The toolbar identifies file, text and Studio exchange operations with icons. Hover or focus a button for its destination. Drag assignments to the scale track in the Studio song they came from.
- Formant Singer follows written katakana in English phrases. A reading such as ハンズ joins the nasal to the final consonant, and ノット keeps its written オ before the final stop.
- The Windows and macOS installers group bundled plug-ins by category and let you select the ones to install. The Windows Portable includes the instruments, effects and demo songs.

## 日本語

ライブセッションでは構成ごとに各トラックのテイクを選び、回数を指定してキューに追加できます。演奏中には現在と次のコードが表示されます。Scale Editorの試聴音源、保存、スケールの受け渡しはヘッダーから操作できます。ボタンにポインターかキーボードフォーカスを合わせると、操作対象と宛先が表示されます。

Windows packages are unsigned. The macOS build targets Apple silicon and is distributed without Developer ID signing or notarization.
